Project: CHARRMPASS
Description: RFID vehicle entry and exit monitoring system.

Hardware:
- RFID reader (RC522 or PN532)
- ESP32 (main controller)
- LCD display (I2C 16x2 or OLED)
- Relay module (for gate/barrier control)
- Power supply: 12V for barrier + 5V for ESP32

Notes:
- Uses 13.56 MHz MIFARE cards
- Stores vehicle RFID tags with registration info
- Logs entry/exit timestamps
- Can trigger a relay to open a gate/barrier
- Future: GSM module for SMS alerts when unknown card detected

Status: Active development
